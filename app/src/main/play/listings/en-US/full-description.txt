Six noises, generated live on your phone, mixed the way you like them.

Sleepy Cocktail plays no recordings. Every sound is synthesized sample by sample while you listen, so nothing loops and nothing repeats — and the app stays a few megabytes instead of a few hundred.

<b>The six noises</b>

- <b>White</b> — the full band, even across every frequency. The classic masker.
- <b>Pink</b> — softer and deeper, the balance most people find restful.
- <b>Brown</b> — a low rumble, the darkest of the three.
- <b>Surf</b> — waves that break at their own pace, never on a fixed period.
- <b>Grey</b> — shaped to the ear's own sensitivity, so the whole band is heard as level.
- <b>Green</b> — the middle of the band, where an outdoor ambience carries its weight.

<b>Mix them</b>

Every noise has its own switch and its own slider. Surf over brown, pink on its own, all six at once — the mix is yours, and the app remembers it.

<b>Sleep timer</b>

Set it in half-hour steps and playback stops on its own. It keeps running when you leave the app or lock the screen, because the sound lives in a background service rather than on the screen.

<b>What this app does not do</b>

- No ads. Not on the screen, not between sounds, not ever.
- No account and no sign-up.
- No sound files to download — everything is generated on the device, so playback needs no connection.

Six languages: English, Arabic, German, Spanish, Russian and Ukrainian. Two dark themes, because a bedside screen should not be a lamp.
